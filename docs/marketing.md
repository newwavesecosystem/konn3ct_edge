# conn3ct — Marketing Plan (Including Social + Paid Campaign)

## 1) Positioning
conn3ct is positioned as:
- **The webinar-first meeting platform** for teams, educators, and creators
- Built for **scale + simplicity** with **monetizable recordings**
- **Africa-first billing** with Paystack (while remaining globally expandable)

One-line pitch:
> Host live sessions at scale, manage your team and rooms, and sell replays—all in one place.

## 2) Primary Use Cases to Market
- Company townhalls and trainings (webinar mode)
- Paid classes/masterclasses + replay sales (paid recordings)
- Community AMAs and product demos
- Remote team meetings with better moderation + recording library

## 3) Target Audiences & Channels

### Audience Segments
- Trainers/educators: cohort-based learning, certifications, exam prep
- Creators/communities: paid live sessions + replay monetization
- SMBs: internal trainings + client webinars

### Channels
Organic:
- X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
- SEO content: “how to host webinars in Nigeria”, “Paystack paid webinar replays”
- Partnerships: training schools, communities, coworking spaces

Paid:
- Meta ads (Instagram/Facebook)
- Google Search (high-intent keywords)
- LinkedIn (B2B webinars/training) if budget allows

## 4) Messaging Pillars
1) **Scale without stress**: webinar mode designed for large audiences
2) **Turn recordings into revenue**: paid replays with Paystack
3) **Team-ready**: invite co-hosts and assistants with roles
4) **Clean UI**: simple join, modern controls, fewer clicks

Proof points (to collect for launch):
- Join success rate and stability stats
- Customer testimonials
- Short demo videos (30–60s)

## 5) Launch Strategy (Phased)

### Phase A: Private Beta (2–4 cohorts)
Goal: validate core experience and messaging.
- Recruit 20–50 hosts (trainers, communities)
- Offer free/discounted Pro plan for feedback
- Gather testimonials + clips

### Phase B: Public Launch
Goal: convert early interest into trials and paid.
- Launch landing page + waitlist
- Publish “Paid replay” feature as headline
- Launch referral program (hosts invite hosts)

### Phase C: Growth Loops
- “Powered by conn3ct” footer on free plan pages (optional)
- Host pages shareable; each share can drive new signups
- Recording pages indexable (SEO) where appropriate

## 7) Paid Campaign Plan (Meta + Google)

### Campaign 1: “Paid Replay” (Creators/Trainers)
Objective:
- Conversions to trial or demo booking
Targeting:
- Interests: online courses, coaching, webinars, digital products
- Lookalikes: website visitors, email list (when available)
Creative angles:
- “Host live → sell replays”
- “Paystack payments included”
Example ad copy:
- Headline: “Sell Your Webinar Replay”
- Primary text: “Run your live class and publish a paid replay link. Collect payments via Paystack.”
Landing page:
- Feature page with 60s demo + pricing + FAQ

### Campaign 2: “Team Trainings” (SMBs)
Objective:
- Leads or website conversions
Targeting:
- Job titles/industries: HR, L&D, founders, ops
Creative angles:
- “Rooms + recordings library”
- “Invite your team as co-hosts”
Example ad copy:
- Headline: “Train Your Team Better”
- Primary text: “Host trainings and keep recordings organized in one place.”

### Campaign 3: Google Search (High Intent)
Keywords (examples):
- “webinar platform paystack”
- “paid webinar replay”
- “host webinar nigeria”
- “online class platform”
Ad groups:
- Paid replay
- Webinar hosting
- Team training
Landing pages:
- Match each ad group to a specific page to improve conversion

## 8) Pricing Page & Offer Design
- Show 4 plans clearly with “best for” labels
- Put “paid recordings” as a Business+ feature
- Include annual discount to improve cashflow

## 9) Conversion & Retention Tactics
- Onboarding checklist:
  - Create room → invite team → schedule first session → publish first recording
- Email drip (trial):
  - Day 0: setup guide
  - Day 2: webinar engagement tips
  - Day 5: “publish paid replay” tutorial
- In-product prompts:
  - After first recorded session: “Publish as free/paid?”

## 10) Metrics to Track
Acquisition:
- CAC by channel, CTR, CVR
Activation:
- Room creation rate, first meeting hosted
Monetization:
- Trial → paid conversion, MRR, paid recording purchase rate
Retention:
- Weekly active hosts, meeting frequency, churn reasons

# Konn3ct: Marketing Team Structure for the 60-Day Hyper-Growth Plan
**Objective:** 10,000,000 Free Users | 6,000 Paying Customers | 60 Days

---

## Executive Summary
To achieve the 60-day hyper-growth targets, Konn3ct requires a **focused, high-velocity marketing team of 12 core personnel**, plus a network of commission-based freelancers (Reseller Army). This is not a brand-awareness campaign—it is a full-scale acquisition war. Every role is designed around a single purpose: getting users in the door and converting them to paying customers as fast as possible.

## 1. Head of Growth Marketing

### Qualifications & Skills
- 5+ years of experience in growth marketing, preferably in SaaS or tech platforms

- Define and distribute weekly OKRs to every team member

- Directly manage the B2B Partnerships Manager and Telco Partnerships Executive
- Weekly reporting to the CEO/Founders on KPI progress vs. the 10M/6K targets
- Identify bottlenecks in the funnel weekly and pivot rapidly (A/B test pricing pages, CTAs, onboarding flows)

|-----|--------|
| Weekly active signups | 100,000+ by Week 4 |
| Free-to-Paid Conversion Rate | ≥ 1.5% of free users |
| Overall Paid Users by Day 60 | 6,000 |
| Cost Per Acquisition (Paid User) | < ₦50,000 |
